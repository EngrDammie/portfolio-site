Installing it

Android blocks APKs from outside the Play Store by default, so the first install needs one permission from you. It takes about thirty seconds.

  1. Download the file. Tap the button above and allow the download to complete rather than switching networks midway.
  2. Tap the downloaded file. Android will say that the source is not allowed. Tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back and install.
  3. Open QuickReceipt. Go to the Settings tab and save your shop name, address, phone and return policy once. Every receipt after that carries them automatically.

Check it is the file I built

Any download from an unknown source deserves a second look. This one can be checked without installing anything.

To compare it, let the download finish, then in a terminal run shasum -a 256 QuickReceipt_base.apk (macOS and Linux) or certutil -hashfile QuickReceipt_base.apk SHA256 (Windows). If the two lines match, the file is exactly the one I built and it has not been altered in transit.

04

What it actually does

Free The app costs nothing, permanently. There is no account to create, no subscription to start, no trial that expires and nothing to buy later. It is a complete tool, given away because I build software for a living and this is the proof.
Receipts Builds an itemised bill with live subtotal, discount and total as you type. Customer name and number, quantity steppers, and one-tap chips for the products that always sell so you are not retyping them.
Sharing Sends the PDF through the normal Android share sheet, so WhatsApp is one tap away. Or prints it straight to a Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or USB thermal or office printer.
Your details Store name, tagline, address, phone, email, currency symbol and return policy are saved once and printed on every receipt. Change them any time in Settings.
Offline Receipts, settings and shortcuts live in a database on the phone. No account, no signal needed, no subscription, and nothing to maintain if the internet goes down.
Today's sales The dashboard totals the day and splits it by payment method, with a searchable history of every receipt so you can re-send one you already gave out.
Currency Naira, dollars, euros, pounds or Ghanaian cedis — whichever you are selling in.
